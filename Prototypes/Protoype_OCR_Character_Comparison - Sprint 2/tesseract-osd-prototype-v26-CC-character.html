RdLn™ Tesseract OCR with OSD Prototype v26-CC-Character

This prototype demonstrates Tesseract.js with Orientation and Script Detection (OSD) for 10 supported languages.

Note: For OSD functionality, the osd.traineddata model must be available. Tesseract.js will automatically download it when needed.

v26-CC-Character Changes: Instead of comparing words, this version compares individual characters. Only count characters that are different between Simplified and Traditional models in the first pass tally. Identical characters are excluded from decision making.
