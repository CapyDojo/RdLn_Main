RdLn™ Tesseract OCR with OSD Prototype v27A-CC

This prototype demonstrates Tesseract.js with Orientation and Script Detection (OSD) for 10 supported languages.

Note: For OSD functionality, the osd.traineddata model must be available. Tesseract.js will automatically download it when needed.

v27A-CC Changes: Implements text-based word matching instead of positional alignment. Only compares words that both models actually detected (eliminates alignment issues when models detect different word counts).

Text-Based Matching Approach (v27A)

How it works:

  1. Extract all unique words from both Simplified and Traditional results
  2. For each unique word, find how each model handled it (confidence scores)
  3. Only count words that both models actually detected (same source word, different interpretations)
  4. Skip alignment issues by matching words by content rather than position

This eliminates synchronization problems when models detect different numbers of words.
