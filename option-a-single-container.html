✅ Pros:

  • Single glassmorphism context for perfect continuity
  • Simpler CSS architecture
  • Better performance (one element vs two)
  • Easier to maintain consistent styling

❌ Cons:

  • Complex clip-path calculations
  • Less flexible for different tab/panel sizes
  • Harder to animate individual components
  • May require recalculation for responsive design
  • Content positioning becomes more complex
