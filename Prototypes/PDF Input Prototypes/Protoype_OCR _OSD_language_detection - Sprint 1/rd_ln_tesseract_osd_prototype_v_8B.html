RdLn™ Tesseract OCR with OSD Prototype v8

v8 removes the prior bias toward the first Simplified pass. It decides Simplified vs Traditional using a symmetric multi‑signal analyzer and exposes richer diagnostics.

🗺️ Dumbed‑Down Flowchart

1️⃣ Run OCR twice → once with Simplified model, once with Traditional model.
2️⃣ Collect both outputs + their confidence scores.
3️⃣ Look for S‑only or T‑only characters (like 体 vs 體).
4️⃣ Convert text back and forth with OpenCC (T→S and S→T) and measure how much changes.
5️⃣ Check for common word pairs (经济 vs 經濟) and punctuation cues (like 「」).
6️⃣ Each clue gives a small vote toward Simplified or Traditional.
7️⃣ Add up the votes → get a final score.
8️⃣ Decision:
  • Score strongly Simplified → choose Simplified output.
  • Score strongly Traditional → choose Traditional output.
  • Too close → mark as “mixed” and use the higher‑confidence output.

Plain‑English steps
  1. Run both models: get texts and confidence from chi_sim and chi_tra.
  2. Collect clues: unique characters, conversion changes (T→S vs S→T), round‑trip stability, word pairs, 「」 quotes, model confidence.
  3. Add the votes: each clue pushes the score toward Traditional (+) or Simplified (−).
  4. Decide: big positive → Traditional; big negative → Simplified; small → Mixed.
  5. Output: pick the matching OCR text (or higher confidence if Mixed).
