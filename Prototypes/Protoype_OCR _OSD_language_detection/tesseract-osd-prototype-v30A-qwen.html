RdLn™ Tesseract OCR with OSD Prototype v30A-qwen

\n

This prototype demonstrates Character-level analysis for Chinese OCR to solve mis-categorization issues between Traditional and Simplified Chinese models.

\n

Note: For OSD functionality, the osd.traineddata model must be available. Tesseract.js will automatically download it when needed.

\n

v30A-qwen Changes: Implemented character-level preservation analysis to solve mis-categorization issues caused by chi-sim and chi-tra engines treating the same text differently.
