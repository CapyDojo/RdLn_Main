RdLn™ Tesseract OCR with OSD Prototype v26B-QC

This prototype demonstrates Tesseract.js with Orientation and Script Detection (OSD) for 10 supported languages.

Note: For OSD functionality, the osd.traineddata model must be available. Tesseract.js will automatically download it when needed.

v26B-QC Changes: Implements spatial proximity matching for character comparison. Instead of positional alignment, characters are matched based on their physical location in the image. Only count characters that are different between Simplified and Traditional models in the first pass tally. Identical characters and punctuation-only characters are excluded from decision making.

Spatial Proximity Matching Approach (v26B)

How it works:

  1. Extract all characters from both Simplified and Traditional models with their bounding boxes
  2. For each character from one model, find the closest character from the other model based on spatial distance
  3. Only compare characters that are within a proximity threshold
  4. Characters without close matches are treated as model-specific detections

This approach solves alignment issues by matching characters based on where they appear in the image rather than their detection order.
