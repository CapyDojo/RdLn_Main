Option C: Organic Intersection

This approach combines radial and linear gradients with varied angles and organic curves to create a more natural, flowing bamboo forest effect. The gradients use different easing patterns to simulate the organic way bamboo grows and intersects in nature.

Elliptical radial gradients add natural bamboo bend effects
